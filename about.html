01 / INTRODUCTION

A builder. A breaker.
An engineer.

I work at the intersection of how applications are built and how they can fail.

I'm Kevin, an Application Security Engineer who likes to get close to the code.

My work spans manual penetration testing, secure code review, API security, and DevSecOps. I investigate the behavior behind a finding, connect it to business risk, and help development teams make the right fix.

At Citi, I work across business-critical web applications, APIs, and backend services. My experience includes deep Java/Spring assessments, white-box testing, threat modeling, security automation, and evaluating AI-driven security tools.

Before Citi, I brought security testing into Jenkins pipelines at NetBrain and completed a master's in cybersecurity at Northeastern. Teaching and mentoring graduate students shaped how I communicate technical findings: clearly, practically, and with the person on the other side in mind.
